# PRICING.md: source of truth for prices and offer copy

Items marked **DECIDE** are proposals. Don't publish them until I confirm.

## Rules for any pricing copy
- All prices in NOK. Add "Not VAT-registered" until I register for mva (required above 50,000 kr sales in 12 months).
- No "normal price" or "you save" text unless I will really charge that price later.
- Don't imply past clients paid a listed price. Use "projects like this start at ...".
- No claims about agencies' prices without a source.
- "No monthly fees" only for packages without a care plan. Otherwise: "No platform fees. Optional care plan."
- Turnaround: "Target 1-2 weeks" until measured builds show faster.

## Terms (one page, plain language)
- 50% before I start, 50% when it's live and tested on their phone.
- Included: 1 revision round and 30 days of fixes. Further changes are batched or covered by the plans.
- Client confirms they own or have rights to the photos, logos and text they send. I only use assets listed in `docs/ASSETS.md`.
- Rights: client gets rights to the finished site and their content once paid in full. Open-source and licensed assets keep their own licences.
- If a care plan lapses: reminder, grace period (**DECIDE**), then the site goes offline. Client can export their content.
- Data: for booking/admin features I sign a data processing agreement with the client. Draft only, review before use.
- No uptime guarantee. I fix issues on a best-effort basis (**DECIDE** response time).

## Payment notes
- Vipps: the client needs a Vipps business agreement. Never suggest a personal Vipps number. Vipps sales to a business count as cash sales, so the client may need an approved cash register. They should check with their accountant.
- Cards: Stripe on the client's own account.
