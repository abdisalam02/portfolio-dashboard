## Rules (from GEMINI.md)

- CC-BY attribution must be visible on page: title, author, link, licence link.
- Never name files after their source (`pin-*.jpg` is a violation — rename after rights are cleared).
- No Pinterest, Instagram, Google Images, TikTok, or hotlinked third-party hosts.
- AI-generated images allowed only if no real people, brands, or celebrities depicted.
- Demos must use a "Demo — fictional business" label.
